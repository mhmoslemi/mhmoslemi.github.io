### 🥣 Nutrition for Triathlon / Long Runs and Rides

Fueling well on the bike and the run is one of the biggest performance levers in long-course triathlon, and it's also one of the easiest things to get wrong. Here's what has worked for me:

- **Carbs:** aim for ~80g of carbohydrate per hour on the bike and run. This can feel rough at first if your gut isn't used to it, so build up to it gradually in training rather than trying it cold on race day.
- **Sodium:** ~700mg of salt per hour has been my personal sweet spot for avoiding cramps and GI issues.
- **Gels:** I like [Maurten](https://www.maurten.com/) gels for carbs, easy to digest at high doses.
- **Electrolytes:** I use [Precision Fuel & Hydration](https://www.precisionfuelandhydration.com/) for electrolytes.

*Disclaimer: this is just based on my own trial and error and what works for my body. Needs can vary quite a bit per individual, so test your own nutrition plan in training before race day.*

If you live in Montreal, [Boutique Endurance](https://www.boutiqueendurance.ca/en) is a great store for running gear and nutrition. For bike service and parts, [Cycles Régis](https://cyclesregis.com/en) is a solid option, though I don't suggest it for nutrition.
